1. Executive summary

MASAREEN is an evidence-first social and economic network. It converts real activities into structured records, verifies them with the institutions that hosted or witnessed them, and keeps the resulting value discoverable for 365 days and beyond.

One sentence: MASAREEN is not an event app, a directory, or a job board. It is the continuity layer that turns a 3-day exhibition into a permanent, verified, monetisable digital economy.

6. Impact measurement: definitions and mechanisms

MASAREEN separates activity, evidence, verification, impact and reach. Reach is attention. Impact is verified change. The platform never presents reach as impact.

6.1 The evidence hierarchy

6.1b Structured evidence, not a link box

Evidence is no longer a single URL field. Every record must name the exact entity it proves and carries structured context that a host or admin can review without leaving MASAREEN.

Each evidence record stores title, description, claim, evidence kind, target, structured fields_json, photos/video/audio/documents in R2, latitude/longitude, claimed location, captured time, source/collector name, witnesses and scan metadata. The mobile capture flow requires GPS and at least one supporting description, file or structured field. A participant record is validated against the event and the signed-in user, so a contributor cannot attach proof to somebody else's contribution.

6.2 Post-level Impact Score Algorithm v2 (0–10 / ISA 0–1000)

The live score is a non-linear, multi-domain model. It is deliberately not a linear sum: every metric saturates, every domain decays at its own rate, and evidence coverage, location proof, actor history, cross-domain consistency and community confirmation are combined multiplicatively. The same verified activity cannot be inflated by one repeated metric.

DomainMetric examplesInitial domain weight
EnvironmentalCO₂ kg, water litres, trees, biodiversity20%
SocialPeople reached, volunteer hours, lives improved, inclusion24%
EconomicIncome EGP, jobs, local GDP contribution16%
GovernanceTransparency, policy adherence, community engagement10%
EducationalStudents reached, learning hours, curriculum, retention16%
InfrastructureFacilities, maintenance hours, people with new access14%
normalized_metric = 1 − exp(−log(1+value) / log(1+k))  [log metrics]
normalized_metric = 1 − exp(−value / k)  [linear metrics]

domain_score = Σ(metric_weight × normalized_metric) / Σ(metric_weight)
temporal_decay(ENV)=e^(−0.05d) · SOC=max(0,1−(min(d,365)/365)²) · GOV=sigmoid(0.05(d−180))
temporal_decay(ECO)=e^(−0.1d) · EDU=e^(−0.03d) · INF=max(0.5,1−0.02d)

coverage = supplied_metrics / 46
consistency = (1 − 0.25×tanh(variance(domain_scores)×6)) × (0.72 + 0.28×coverage)
geo = 1.00 (no GPS) · 1.06 (GPS) · 1.12 (GPS + claimed location)
actor = 1 + 0.5×e^(−verified_actions/50)
bayes_rating = (avg_stars×n + 3×2) / (n+2) / 5
engagement = tanh(log(1 + likes×2 + comments×2.5 + shares×3) / log(61))
community = 0.65×bayes_rating + 0.35×engagement

raw = Σ(domain_score × domain_weight × decay) × geo × actor × consistency × (0.82 + 0.18×community)
impact = 10 × (1 − e^(−2.4×raw))
ISA = round(impact × 100) → 0–1000

GPS is part of the evidence, not decoration. Structured impact/evidence posts require a captured location. If the author also states the place, the post reaches geo level 2 (GPS + claimed location) and receives the higher factor; a host can reject a mismatch during verification. GPS is not used to secretly track members: it is stored on the post and shown in the evidence context.

confidence = clamp(22 + coverage×35 + min(15, voters×3)
  + (author_verified ? 15 : 0) + (GPS ? 5 : 0) + engagement×8, 5, 99)
OutputRuleMeaning
Awaiting ratingsISA 0No impact signal yet.
Early signalimpact < 4.5Some traction, weak evidence.
Emerging impact4.5 ≤ impact < 6.5Credible but limited confirmation.
Strong impact6.5 ≤ impact < 8Strong multi-domain evidence and confirmation.
Verified high impactimpact ≥ 8 and confidence ≥ 70High evidence, high confidence, verified author.

Legacy posts without structured metrics fall back to the earlier weighted rating/engagement formula; they are automatically upgraded the next time a structured rating is submitted after migration 029.

Why this matters: a viral post with no verification can score high on reach but cannot earn the “Verified high impact” label, cannot be externally shared, and does not count as a verified submission for PMS or trust. A single metric cannot buy impact: saturation, domain decay and consistency penalties are intentional.

6.2b Home-feed ranking, stochasticity and paid promotion

The home feed is a relevance-ranked stream with per-session stochasticity. Each refresh creates a new seed; pagination keeps the same seed so pages never repeat or skip. This directly fixes the “same posts again and again” problem while preserving stable infinite scroll.

base = 0.34×impact + 0.18×verified + 0.18×recency + 0.14×velocity
  + 0.08×following + 0.05×interest + 0.03×media + 0.02×self
seeded_explore = hash(seed, post) × 0.30 + hash(seed, post, b) × 0.14
paid_boost = active boost ? clamp(boost_weight, 0, 0.38) × 1.4 : 0
final = base + seeded_explore + paid_boost

author_diversity_penalty = 0.22 × prior_posts_by_author
promoted_slots = max(1, round(limit × 15%))
  • Paid content is a percentage, not a takeover: active promoted posts are reserved about 15% of feed slots (at least one per page when available) and receive a bounded score boost. Ads are already served as separate paid slots with frequency caps.
  • Boost with MRC: POST /api/v1/posts/:id/boost debits 5–5000 MRC, promotes for 1–30 days, and records paid_mrc, boost_weight and boosted_until. A 25 MRC boost buys one day; 150 MRC buys six days at a stronger weight.
  • Fairness: paid boost cannot bypass missing evidence, moderation, sharing restrictions or author verification. It only affects discovery ranking.
Measured result: two seeded requests seed=alpha and seed=beta produce different orders, while repeating seed=alpha produces exactly the same order for pagination.

6.2c Deepfake and NSFW authenticity gate

Authenticity is reviewed before a host can verify evidence. The gate is free-tier by design: local forensic scoring always runs, and Cloudflare Workers AI is used when the AI binding is available. If the model call fails, the local result still protects the pipeline.

SignalHow it is checkedEffect
AI-text patternsFormulaic phrasing, punctuation regularity, sentence-length variance and generator markers.Raises the AI-content score; suspicious text enters review.
Generator metadataFilename, URL and embedded markers for Midjourney, DALL-E, Stable Diffusion, Firefly and similar tools.Raises the AI-content score for the attached media.
Image safetyCloudflare Workers AI vision checks the first image for NSFW content; local text markers cover captions and descriptions.Explicit content is blocked with nsfw_blocked.
Evidence scanA scan is stored on every post, evidence record, submission and event contribution.Verdict and score are visible to the reviewer and in admin operations.
ai_score = max(local_text_forensics, media_url_markers, first_image_forensics, cloud_ai)
AI verdict: LIKELY_AI at score ?%? 0.80 ?? SUSPICIOUS at score ?%? 0.55 ?? LIKELY_AUTHENTIC
NSFW verdict: BLOCKED at score ?%? 0.80 ?? REVIEW at score ?%? 0.50 ?? SAFE
  • Public scan endpoint: POST /api/v1/ai/deepfake/scan accepts text and media URLs; admins use POST /api/v1/admin/ai/deepfake/scan for an audited review.
  • Verification pipeline: DEEPFAKE_NSFW_SCAN is the first step. A LIKELY_AI verdict forces HUMAN_REVIEW and lowers confidence to 12; SUSPICIOUS lowers it to 25.
  • No silent approval: verifying flagged evidence or a flagged event contribution returns 409 deepfake_review_required. A host or admin may explicitly override with override_deepfake: true after reviewing the original files; the override is recorded.
  • Privacy: only the evidence media needed for the check is fetched; the scan does not create a separate identity or tracking profile.
Free and defensible: local forensics work without any paid vendor. Cloudflare Workers AI adds vision and safety checks inside the existing free allocation, and every verdict stores which provider produced it.

6.2d Mixed-entity discovery and infinite feed

The home feed is no longer a post-only stream. After the ranked posts, the app appends discovery cards for every public entity type: events, programmes, projects, opportunities, podcasts and VOD. Each card carries an explicit impact percentage, verification state, cover media and a direct route into the entity.

  • One stream, many entities: GET /api/v1/discovery/feed merges six entity tables and round-robins them so one type cannot dominate the page.
  • Percentages are honest: verified events show their verification strength, approved programmes/projects use their review state, and podcasts/VOD carry their own score. The UI never labels reach as impact.
  • Infinite by design: posts paginate with a stable seed; when posts are exhausted, discovery pages continue. Exhausted discovery pages rotate a new seed and keep loading, so the feed has no terminal “finished” state.
  • Dark mode: a one-tap sun/moon control in the feed app bar and the drawer switch persist the choice locally; cards, tabs, forms and galleries use the active colour scheme.

6.2e Data entry is a screen, not a URL box

Programme, project, opportunity, podcast show, episode, VOD, booth, event and course creation now use full screens with descriptive fields and device uploads. Users choose a cover photo, audio file or video from their phone; the app uploads it to R2 and stores the returned URL. No image URL is requested from a member. Fields include the context a reviewer needs: lead organisation, category, SDG, dates, location, beneficiaries, budget, skills, guests, duration and success criteria.

6.2f Trusted operations: moderators, notifications, documents and help

Entities can assign scoped moderators with explicit permissions: remove comments, remove posts, verify contributions, remove the event or manage booths. A moderator acts only inside the assigned entity and never inherits platform-admin rights. Owners and moderators can block accounts, remove comments and posts, and remove an event from public discovery.

Notifications are high-signal by design. Social likes, follows and low-value activity stay in the in-app inbox; messages, verification decisions, moderation, tasks, event decisions, leads, funding and platform announcements can push. Pushes are deduplicated, capped to four per ten minutes, and suppressed when the member chooses a digest or quiet hours.

PDF and DOCX attachments open inside the app: PDFs render natively, Word files are parsed as XML text with control characters removed and a size limit. The Help Center explains the loop, verification, impact model, moderators, documents, safety and sharing with diagrams, while four HELP posts introduce the platform directly in the feed.

6.3 Verification states

Event contributions, submissions and evidence move through: SUBMITTED → UNDER_REVIEW → VERIFIED or REJECTED. Every state change records actor and timestamp. Hosts cannot verify their own contribution. Admin override is audited.

9. Event hubs, contributors, booths and leads

9.1 The five-stage flow

  1. Create: organizer creates the event, adds host, exhibitors, sponsors, speakers, students and media; links the programme.
  2. Document: contributors submit contribution summaries and evidence links; event media, podcasts and VOD are attached.
  3. Verify: the host reviews evidence, approves/rejects, and the event becomes a verified activity; MRC is issued.
  4. Cross-link: the verified event appears on organizer, host, exhibitor, speaker, student and media profiles; entity links make it discoverable in both directions.
  5. Year-round interaction: booths collect quotes/trials/leads, visitors rate, organizers read dashboards, and the event keeps producing opportunity.

9.2 Booths and lead privacy

Each booth has products, media, a CTA type (quote, trial, book, contact), optional trial price, unique views, interactions and leads. Leads are visible only to the booth owner and the event organizer (and admins). A visitor receives 2 MRC for a verified experience; a paid trial moves MRC atomically from visitor to exhibitor.

14. Verified-only external sharing policy

External sharing means copying a public link, invoking the OS share sheet, or otherwise moving MASAREEN content outside the app. It is a verified-content privilege.

17. Security, privacy and PDPL

  • Auth: PBKDF2-SHA256 passwords (100,000 iterations), signed session tokens, secure storage on device, email verification, magic links, Google sign-in.
  • Authorization: ownership checks on entities, booths, leads, participants and dashboards; admin-only routes; self-verification blocked.
  • Privacy: lead lists private to owner/organizer; profile visibility settings; show-email toggle; message privacy; block/report; data deletion.
  • PDPL: explicit consent at registration; de-identified aggregated reports for sponsors; purpose limitation; data minimisation in public JSON; audit logs.
  • Integrity: rate limits, upload size limits, moderation, CSV injection protection, atomic wallet operations, idempotent rewards.
  • Media: public R2 with controlled upload paths; HLS through edge proxy; no tunnel credentials in the client.
