# 🌟 MASAREN User Guide: Your Journey to Impact

Welcome to **MASAREN (مسارين)**. If you've never heard of us, you are about to discover a platform that turns your real-world good deeds into a digital asset.

## ❓ What is MASAREN?
In the real world, people do amazing things-helping the poor, planting trees, teaching children, or managing huge social projects. Usually, this "impact" is invisible. It exists in memories or old paper reports.

**MASAREN changes that.** We provide the "Infrastructure for Impact." We help you verify your field work using AI and GPS, and then we convert that verified work into a **Reputation Score (PMS/OII)**. This score is like a "Credit Score for Goodness"-it proves your value to the world.

---

## 🛠 How to Use the Platform

### 1. Getting Started
- **Register**: Create your account. Choose if you are an **Individual** (professional/volunteer) or an **Organization** (NGO, Club, Government Agency).
- **Build Your Profile**: Add your skills, your nationality, and the causes you care about (SDGs).

### 2. The "Two Paths" Workflow
MASAREN works in two phases: **Intention** and **Execution**.

#### Path A: Intention (The Marketplace)
- Browse the **Opportunities** tab.
- Find a task or project that matches your skills and a "Minimum PMS Score."
- Apply for the opportunity. Once accepted, it becomes a "Task" on your dashboard.

#### Path B: Execution (The Field Work)
- Go to the field and do the work.
- **Capture Evidence**: Use the MASAREN Mobile App to take photos, record audio, or upload documents.
- **GPS Verification**: The app automatically logs your location to prove you were actually at the site.
- **AI Verification**: Our AI agents analyze your evidence to ensure it's real and high-quality.

### 3. Your Impact Passport
Once your work is verified, it's added to your **Impact Passport**.
- **PMS (Personal Merit Score)**: Your individual score of trust and capability.
- **OII (Org Impact Index)**: The score for organizations based on their collective success.
- **Timeline**: A beautiful, visual history of every verified action you've ever taken.

## 💰 Paid Services & Ads
To keep the platform running, we offer:
- **Express Verification**: Pay a small fee to get your evidence reviewed faster.
- **Sponsored Opportunities**: Organizations can pay to have their job/funding posts appear at the top of the feed.
- **Premium Profiles**: Enhanced visualization for high-impact professionals.

If you get stuck, check the **Educational Posts** in your feed or contact the MASAREN Admin team through the support portal.
