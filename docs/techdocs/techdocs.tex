\section{Executive Summary}

This financial report provides a comprehensive cost analysis of Artificial Intelligence (AI) services deployed on the MASAREN Impact Infrastructure as a Service (IIaaS) platform. The analysis is scoped exclusively to cloud-based AI model inference costs---the platform operator provides all other infrastructure (servers, databases, networking, storage) at no incremental AI-related cost.

\subsection{Scope of This Report}

This report covers the following AI services that operate within the MASAREN platform:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
    \item \textbf{Vision AI Service} -- Image/video evidence analysis and verification
    \item \textbf{Fraud Detection Service} -- Geospatial and behavioral fraud scoring
    \item \textbf{Impact Economist Agent} -- Social value computation and SDG scoring
    \item \textbf{Graph Synthesis Service} -- Knowledge graph construction and PMS delta analysis
    \item \textbf{Chat Assistant Service} -- User-facing conversational AI support
    \item \textbf{Verification Orchestrator} -- Multi-agent CrewAI verification pipeline
    \item \textbf{Report Generator Service} -- Narrative synthesis for impact reports
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Out of scope:} Server hosting, database operations, Kafka message broker, Redis cache, Qdrant vector store, CDN, domain/DNS, SSL certificates, monitoring (Prometheus/Grafana), and all non-AI application logic.

\subsection{AI Agent Event Pipeline}

The AI services operate as an event-driven pipeline via Apache Kafka:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=2em]
    \item \textbf{EvidenceUploaded} triggers Vision AI and Fraud Detection in parallel
    \item \textbf{EvidenceVerified} triggers Impact Economist Agent
    \item \textbf{ImpactAssetCreated} triggers Graph Synthesis and Broker Agent
    \item \textbf{VerificationRequested} triggers Verification Orchestrator (CrewAI)
    \item \textbf{ReportGenerated} triggers Report Generator for narrative synthesis
\end{enumerate}

Each event triggers exactly one LLM inference call per consumer, enabling precise cost-per-event calculation.

\section{Business Terms Glossary (Financial Audit Reference)}

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\footnotesize
\begin{tabular}{|p{3.5cm}|p{9.5cm}|}
\hline
\textbf{Term} & \textbf{Definition} \\
\hline
MAU (Monthly Active Users) & Unique users who perform at least one action per calendar month \\
\hline
Token & Minimum unit of text processed by an LLM; approximately 4 characters of English text \\
\hline
Input Tokens & Tokens sent to the LLM as part of a prompt (user message, system instructions, context) \\
\hline
Output Tokens & Tokens generated by the LLM as a response \\
\hline
Cost per Token (EGP) & Monthly AI cost divided by total monthly tokens processed \\
\hline
Cost per User/Month (EGP) & Monthly AI cost divided by number of active users \\
\hline
Prompt Caching & Technique to store and reuse frequently sent prompt prefixes, reducing input token costs by 50--75\% \\
\hline
Model Fallback & Automatic switching to a cheaper or more available model when the primary model is unavailable or rate-limited \\
\hline
Rate Limiting & Restriction on the number of API calls per time window to prevent cost overruns \\
\hline
Quota & Maximum allocated token/call limit per user per time period \\
\hline
Overage & Usage exceeding the allocated quota, charged at a premium rate \\
\hline
User-Charged Model & Billing structure where AI costs are passed to end users rather than absorbed by the platform operator \\
\hline
Cost Recovery Rate & Percentage of AI service costs recovered through user fees; target is 100\% \\
\hline
Gross Margin & Revenue minus AI costs, expressed as a percentage of revenue \\
\hline
Break-Even Point & Number of paying users required for AI cost revenue to equal AI service expenses \\
\hline
Volume Discount & Pricing reduction offered by AI providers for high-volume usage commitments \\
\hline
Embedding & Numerical vector representation of text for semantic search; generated by embedding models \\
\hline
Latency (p50/p95/p99) & Response time percentiles; p95 = 95\% of requests complete within this time \\
\hline
CrewAI & Multi-agent AI framework used for complex verification workflows \\
\hline
LLM (Large Language Model) & Foundation model capable of text generation, analysis, and reasoning \\
\hline
EGP & Egyptian Pound; primary currency for all financial projections in this report \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\caption{Business Terms Glossary for Financial Audit}
\end{table}
