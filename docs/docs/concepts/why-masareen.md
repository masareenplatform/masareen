# Why MASAREEN Exists

## The problem we are solving

The social-impact sector moves trillions of dollars and billions of volunteer hours every
year — and yet it runs on **self-reporting**. An NGO's annual report is written by the NGO.
A volunteer's CV lists work no one can confirm. A government program claims outcomes no one
audited at the ground level.

Three failure modes follow:

1. **Frictionless fraud.** Without grounded proof, false or inflated claims are cheap and
   common.
2. **Wasted capital.** Donors can't tell effective implementers from effective marketers, so
   money flows to the loudest, not the best.
3. **Burnout & invisibility.** The people doing the real work get no durable, portable
   record of it. Their reputation resets at every new organization.

## The shift we enable

MASAREEN replaces *assertion* with *attestation*:

| Before | After MASAREEN |
|--------|----------------|
| "We planted 10,000 trees." | GPS-stamped photos + AI media check + human audit per plot. |
| CV lists volunteer work. | Portable **impact passport** with verified entries. |
| Donor trusts a brand. | Donor trusts a **verification pipeline** with a verdict trail. |
| Reputation stuck in one org. | **PMS/OII** scores travel across the network. |

## Who it is for

- **Individuals & professionals** who want their real-world good work to count — and to
  unlock better opportunities.
- **NGOs & clubs** that need trustworthy proof of outcomes for funders.
- **Government agencies** that must demonstrate program impact transparently.
- **Companies** running CSR / ESG programs and seeking verifiable social ROI.
- **Donors & partners** who want to allocate capital by verified impact, not narrative.

## Why now

Cheap AI vision, ubiquity of GPS-enabled smartphones, and mature verification primitives
finally make ground-level attestation practical. MASAREEN is the layer that composes them
into something a volunteer in a village and a funder in a capital can both trust.

## The name

**MASAREEN (مسارين)** — *two paths*. Impact is a loop between **intention** (finding and
accepting work) and **execution** (doing it and proving it). We named the platform after
that loop because the loop *is* the product.
