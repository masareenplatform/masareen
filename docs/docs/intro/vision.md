# Vision & Design Principles

MASAREEN is not a social network with a cause bolted on. It is **infrastructure** — the
plumbing that lets impact be proven, scored, and exchanged. Everything below flows from
that single decision.

## The thesis

Trust in the impact sector is broken in three ways:

1. **Unverifiable claims.** Anyone can say they planted 10,000 trees. Almost no one can
   prove it a year later.
2. **Fragmented reputation.** A great volunteer in Cairo has no portable record of their
   work that a donor in Berlin can trust.
3. **No feedback loop.** Doing good rarely *unlocks* more good — there is no mechanism that
   rewards verified impact with new opportunities, funding, or visibility.

MASAREEN closes all three loops. Verified evidence becomes a **reputation asset**, and that
asset is the currency of the network.

## The two-path name

**MASAREEN (مسارين)** means *"two paths"* in Arabic. The platform is deliberately built
around two phases that mirror how real impact actually happens:

- **Path A — Intention (the Marketplace).** You discover *opportunities*: tasks, programs,
  and calls for help posted by organizations. You apply; if accepted, the opportunity
  becomes a *task* assigned to you.
- **Path B — Execution (the Field).** You go do the work and **capture evidence**. The
  evidence runs through a verification pipeline. When it passes, it is scored and feeds
  your reputation.

The two paths are not sequential screens — they are a **continuous loop**. Reputation earned
in Execution unlocks better opportunities in Intention, which produces more verified evidence.

## Principles

- **Verify, don't trust.** Self-reporting is a starting point, never proof. Evidence is
  checked by AI and humans before it counts.
- **Identity is plural by design.** A person is a volunteer *and* an employee *and* a donor.
  An organization is an NGO *and* a government agency *and* a company partner. MASAREEN
  models multiple profile identities (see [Profiles & Identities](/docs/concepts/profiles-and-identities)).
- **Impact is portable.** Your reputation score travels with you across orgs, programs, and
  even outside MASAREEN via an *impact passport*.
- **Transparency is the product.** Every verified claim is auditable: who, what, where
  (GPS), when, and who verified it.
- **The network effect is the point.** Matching, recommendations, and graphs connect people
  to opportunities and organizations to talent they could never find alone.

## What success looks like

A municipality posts a "clean the canal" opportunity. Fifty residents apply; the matching
service ranks them by PMS score and skill. Ten are accepted and complete tasks, each
capturing GPS-stamped photos. The verification pipeline clears the evidence. Their PMS
scores rise, the municipality's OII (Organization Impact Index) rises, and the next
opportunity auto-suggests the now-more-trusted residents. Impact compounds — visibly.
