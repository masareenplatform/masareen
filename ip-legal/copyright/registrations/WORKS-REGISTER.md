## Purpose

This register is the authoritative record of all original works created under the MASAREN project that qualify for copyright protection. It serves as the foundation for:

- External copyright registration applications
- IP due diligence for investors and partners
- Enforcement of proprietary rights against infringement
- License management and royalty tracking

All works listed herein are owned exclusively by the **MASAREN Project** under the principle of work made for hire. Protection attaches automatically upon creation under the Berne Convention (no registration required), though formal registration is recommended for enhanced enforcement rights.
