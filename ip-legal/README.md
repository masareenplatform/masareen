## About MASAREN

**MASAREN** (مسارين)، *Impact Infrastructure as a Service (IIaaS)*

MASAREN is the world's first Impact Infrastructure Platform, converting every verified real-world field action into immutable, measurable, and economically valuable digital assets. Founded in 2026, MASAREN operates under the core principle:

> *"If an action cannot be verified, it cannot become an economic asset."*

The platform is built on proprietary technology spanning 35+ microservices (Python/FastAPI), Flutter mobile, React web, and a hybrid AI pipeline (GPT-4o / Gemini) orchestrating multi-modal evidence verification, geo-fenced field operations, and a Knowledge Graph-backed impact scoring engine.

## Purpose of This Directory

This `ip-legal/` directory is the **single authoritative source** for all intellectual property (IP) legal documentation pertaining to MASAREN. It is organized to support:

- Active IP protection and registration across all jurisdictions
- Due diligence for funding rounds, partnerships, and audits
- Onboarding of internal legal counsel and external IP attorneys
- Ongoing IP governance, compliance training, and policy enforcement
- Evidence preservation for litigation defense or enforcement proceedings

All materials here are maintained as living documents. Each subdirectory has its own scope and access protocol defined within its section below.
