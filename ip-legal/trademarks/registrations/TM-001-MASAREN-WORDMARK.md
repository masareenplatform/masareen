## 9. DISTINCTIVENESS ANALYSIS

The mark MASAREN is inherently distinctive for the following reasons:

1. **Arbitrary / Fanciful in English-Speaking Markets:** "MASAREN" has no recognized meaning in English and therefore functions as an arbitrary mark with respect to the claimed services in English-speaking jurisdictions.

2. **Transliterated Arabic Term:** While the mark transliterates to مسارين (Two Paths) in Arabic, the romanized form "MASAREN" is not a dictionary word in the Roman-script-dominant markets where protection is primarily sought (USA, EU, UK). The USPTO and EUIPO generally evaluate marks based on the impression they create for the relevant consuming public in those jurisdictions.

3. **No Descriptive Significance:** The mark does not describe any feature, quality, or characteristic of the goods and services listed. "MASAREN" does not describe software, platforms, impact measurement, or any related service.

4. **Secondary Meaning Argument (Backup):** In the event any examining authority determines the mark is merely descriptive (unlikely), MASAREN has acquired secondary meaning as of its first commercial use on 2026-08-11 and will continue to build recognition among relevant consumers.
