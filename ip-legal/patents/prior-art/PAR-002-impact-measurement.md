## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This report surveys prior art in impact measurement, sustainability scoring, and impact quantification methodologies. The analysis covers ESG scoring frameworks, carbon credit standards, social impact measurement tools, and algorithmic impact scoring systems, evaluating each against the MASAREN Impact Score Algorithm (ISA).

**Key Finding:** No existing system combines multi-domain impact scoring with dynamic weight learning, geographic vulnerability adjustment, and blockchain asset value mapping. This represents a clear patentable opportunity for MASAREN.

## 3. EXISTING SOLUTIONS ANALYSIS

### 3.1 ESG Scoring Frameworks

| System | Approach | Limitations | Patent Status |
|--------|----------|-------------|---------------|
| **MSCI ESG Ratings** | Manual analyst assessment + data aggregation | Static weights; 6-8 week lag | Proprietary methodology |
| **Sustainalytics** | Risk-based scoring for 37k+ companies | Corporate focus only; no field-level verification | Proprietary methodology |
| **Refinitiv ESG Score** | Data-driven scoring from public sources | No real-world evidence verification | Proprietary methodology |
| **S&P Global ESG** | Similar to MSCI | Corporate focus; no individual impact | Proprietary methodology |

**Freedom-to-Operate Status:** All use manual or static-weight approaches; no dynamic learning or geographic weighting.

### 3.2 Carbon Credit / Environmental Impact Standards

| System | Approach | Limitations | Patent Status |
|--------|----------|-------------|---------------|
| **Verra (VCS)** | Third-party audit of carbon projects | Manual; months of lag; no real-time scoring | No patents found |
| **Gold Standard** | Similar to Verra | Manual verification | No patents found |
| **Nori** | Carbon removal marketplace on blockchain | Relies on third-party MRV (Measurement, Reporting, Verification) | No patents found |
| **Toucan Protocol** | Carbon credit tokenization | Depends on external verification standards | No patents found |
| **Climate Action Reserve** | GHG offset standards | Regulatory filing approach | No patents found |

**Freedom-to-Operate Status:** No dynamic algorithmic scoring with real-time adjustment.

### 3.3 Social Impact Measurement

| System | Approach | Limitations | Patent Status |
|--------|----------|-------------|---------------|
| **IRIS+ (GIIRS)** | Standardized impact metrics catalog | Descriptive only; no scoring algorithm | Open standard |
| **B Impact Assessment** | Multi-stakeholder scoring (workers, community, environment) | Static weight categories; self-assessment | No patents found |
| **Social Value International** | Social return on investment (SROI) | Manual calculation; ratios not algorithmic | No patents found |
| **Acumen** | Lean data approach | Qualitative; small-sample verification | No patents found |

**Freedom-to-Operate Status:** No algorithmic normalization, dynamic weights, or blockchain value mapping.

| Patent | Owner | Approach | Limitations |
|--------|-------|----------|-------------|
| US-9,852,423 | IBM | Environmental impact scoring via sensor data | Single domain; no cross-domain fusion |
| US-10,546,278 | Microsoft | Sustainability score computation | Cloud infrastructure focus; corporate only |

| US-2023-0085431 | Mastercard | Impact-weighted transaction scoring | Financial transactions; not field actions |
| US-2022-0395671 | PwC | ESG data aggregation and scoring | Data aggregation; no scoring algorithm |
| US-11,574,022 | IBM | Trust-based supply chain scoring | Supply chain context; no real-world impact |
