## CLAIMS

### Independent Claim 1
> A computer-implemented method for converting verified real-world field actions into digital economic assets, the method comprising:
>
> receiving, from a field actor's mobile device, evidence data comprising:
> &nbsp;&nbsp;a photographic image associated with a field action;
> &nbsp;&nbsp;geolocation coordinates associated with the field action;
> &nbsp;&nbsp;device telemetry data from sensors of the mobile device;
>
> subjecting the evidence data to a multi-stage verification pipeline, wherein the multi-stage verification pipeline comprises:
> &nbsp;&nbsp;applying a convolutional neural network to detect image manipulation or generative AI artifacts;
> &nbsp;&nbsp;cross-validating the geolocation coordinates against geospatial boundaries stored in a PostGIS database;
> &nbsp;&nbsp;analyzing the device telemetry data to compute a behavioral authenticity score;
> &nbsp;&nbsp;computing an aggregate verification confidence score from the image authenticity, geolocation validity, and behavioral authenticity;
>
> determining that the aggregate verification confidence score exceeds a threshold value;
>
> computing, via a multi-dimensional impact scoring algorithm, impact metrics associated with the verified field action; and
>
> minting, on a blockchain network, a non-fungible digital token representing the verified field action, wherein the digital token comprises:
> &nbsp;&nbsp;a unique asset identifier;
> &nbsp;&nbsp;metadata encoding the verification confidence score and the impact metrics;
> &nbsp;&nbsp;ownership assignment to the field actor's digital impact portfolio.

### Dependent Claim 2
> The method of claim 1, wherein the multi-stage verification pipeline further comprises performing real-time trajectory validation using continuous GPS monitoring during evidence capture, and wherein the trajectory validation checks for geofence crossing events and speed consistency.

### Dependent Claim 3
> The method of claim 1, wherein the convolutional neural network is trained on a composite dataset comprising synthetic images generated by one or more generative AI models, authentic real-world photographs, and digitally manipulated images, and wherein the neural network outputs a forgery probability score and a manipulation type classification.

### Dependent Claim 4
> The method of claim 1, wherein the aggregate verification confidence score is computed by a cross-modal fusion layer that penalizes inconsistencies between the image authenticity score, geolocation validity score, and behavioral authenticity score.

### Dependent Claim 5
> The method of claim 1, wherein minting the non-fungible digital token further comprises storing the impact metrics and verification provenance in a Neo4j knowledge graph, and wherein the knowledge graph links the digital token to a plurality of related entities including other actors, prior actions, and organizational programs.

### Dependent Claim 6
> The method of claim 1, further comprising:
> &nbsp;&nbsp;monitoring the blockchain for secondary market transactions of the digital token;
> &nbsp;&nbsp;adjusting future impact scoring weights based on market transaction prices; and
> &nbsp;&nbsp;providing feedback to the multi-dimensional impact scoring algorithm for continuous learning.

### Dependent Claim 7
> The method of claim 1, wherein the blockchain network is an EVM-compatible network and the non-fungible digital token conforms to the ERC-721 standard.

### Dependent Claim 8
> The method of claim 1, further comprising emitting a verifiable credential signed by the field actor, the verifiable credential comprising a Merkle root hash of the evidence data, enabling third-party verification of the token's authenticity.

## ABSTRACT (for Publication)

A system and method for verifying real-world field actions and converting them into blockchain-backed digital assets. Evidence from mobile devices (images, GPS, sensor data) is processed through an AI-powered multi-stage verification pipeline that detects forgery, validates geolocation, and computes a behavioral authenticity score. Verified actions are scored using a multi-dimensional impact algorithm and minted as non-fungible tokens linked to the actor's digital impact portfolio.
