Verified Evidence, What It Actually Is

"Verified evidence" is the central noun of MASAREEN. This page defines it precisely, because the whole reputation system rests on getting this right.

Definition​

Verified evidence = a captured artifact (photo, document, audio, GPS trace) that has passed a verification pipeline and is therefore accepted as proof that a specific unit of work happened, where and when it was claimed.

It is not "a photo you uploaded." It is "a photo that an AI and/or human reviewer confirmed is authentic, on-site, and relevant to the task."

The evidence lifecycle​

capture (mobile)  →  upload (media slot)  →  record (evidence entity)
        │
        ▼
attach to Submission  →  start Pipeline  →  AI review  →  (human review?)  →  Verdict
        │
        ▼
VALIDATED evidence  →  feeds Task.verify + PMS + Portfolio + Graph

1. Capture​

The mobile app lets you capture: photo, document, audio, or GPS. GPS is captured automatically on relevant actions (POST /gps/capture, uploaded via /gps/uploaded) so the place is proven, not just the thing.

2. Upload & record​

Media goes to a signed upload slot (POST /api/v1/media/upload-slot), then the asset is recorded (POST /api/v1/media/{asset_id} completion, or POST /evidence/). The evidence service owns the evidence entity (GET /evidence/{evidence_id}).

3. Submission​

Evidence is bundled into a submission (POST /submissions) tied to a task and actor.

4. Pipeline (the verification)​

As detailed in Workflow & Pipelines, the pipeline runs AI review then optional human review and lands on a verdict (POST /api/v1/submissions/{id}/validate or .../reject).

5. Becoming "verified"​

Once validated, the evidence is verified evidence. It is immutable proof: who, what, where (GPS), when, and who verified it (AI confidence + reviewer id). You can always pull the pipeline trail (GET /api/v1/verification/pipelines/evidence/{evidence_id}).

What makes evidence trustworthy​

SignalHow it's checked
AuthenticityAI media analysis detects edits, duplicates, stock photos, mismatched metadata.
LocationGPS vs. the opportunity/task's claimed location; proximity scored.
RelevanceAI reads the artifact against the task description (does this photo show the work?).
TimelinessCapture timestamp vs. task window.
IndependenceWhere possible, corroborating evidence (e.g., a second witness, an audit event).

Fraud scoring​

A dedicated fraud scorer (POST /fraud/score) and the audit service (/api/v1/audit/*) provide independent risk signals. High fraud scores route evidence to human review and can flag an account for the admin queue (/admin/pending).

Why "verified" is durable​

Verified evidence is the leaf node of trust in MASAREEN:

  • It powers the PMS (your personal merit) and OII (org impact) scores.
  • It becomes an asset in your portfolio (GET /api/v1/portfolio/{user_id}/assets).
  • It produces a portable impact passport entry (GET /api/v1/users/{user_id}/impact-passport), proof you can take anywhere.
  • It writes an edge in the graph (POST /api/v1/graph/relationships) linking you to the program/SDG you impacted.

In one sentence: verified evidence is the atomic unit of trust in MASAREEN, and reputation is just the sum of verified evidence you've produced.
