Quickstart

Get from zero to a verified piece of impact in ~10 minutes.

1. Open the platform​

Go to https://masareen.mediahubnetwork.net. You can use the web app immediately, or install the Android app from the landing page download link.

2. Create an account​

  • Click Create an account (web) or open the app and tap Create an account.
  • Enter email, full name, phone, password. Pick INDIVIDUAL and audience PROFESSIONAL (or VOLUNTEER).
  • With the demo config, your account is auto-verified — you can sign in right away.

3. Sign in​

Use email + password, or Continue with Google (after the redirect URI is registered in Google Cloud).

4. Build your profile​

  • Add your skills and the SDGs/causes you care about.
  • Upload a photo / set your bio. This drives matching.

5. Find an opportunity​

  • Go to the Opportunities / marketplace and browse.
  • The matching service ranks opportunities by your reputation + skills. Apply to one.

6. Do the work & capture evidence (mobile)​

  • On the app, open the assigned task and tap start.
  • Do the field work. Capture a photo and let the app log GPS.
  • Submit. This creates a submission and starts a verification pipeline.

7. Get verified​

  • The pipeline runs AI review, then (if needed) human review.
  • When it passes, the task is verified, your PMS goes up, and the work appears in your portfolio and impact passport.

8. Watch the loop close​

Higher PMS → better-matched, higher-tier opportunities become available. Repeat.
